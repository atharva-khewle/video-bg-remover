FAQ

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your video never leaves your device.

Is it really free? Any watermark?

Free, no account, no watermark.

Which video formats are supported?

MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, AVI, FLV, WMV, GIF and more. Formats your browser cannot play are converted automatically (a ~30 MB converter downloads once).

How do I get a transparent background video?

Keep the background on Transparent and export as WebM. WebM keeps the alpha channel; MP4 cannot.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, on modern mobile browsers. Chrome on Android works best. Export runs in real time and needs the tab open, so keep clips short on phones.

Why does export take as long as the video?

The cutout is recorded live while the video plays, which also keeps audio in sync.
